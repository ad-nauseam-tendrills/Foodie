Create an account

Sets a temporary password directly -- no invite link, no email. The person is forced to change it the first time they sign in.

At least 14 characters. No forced symbols/digits, but avoid common words and repeated patterns.

Invite someone

The other way to get someone an account -- generates a link that lets them pick their own email and password, rather than you setting a temporary one for them. No email is sent by the app; copy the link and send it yourself however you like.
